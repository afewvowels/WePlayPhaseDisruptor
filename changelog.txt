---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Version: 0.2.0
Date: 2026-11-12
  Info:
    - First public release.
  Features:
    - Phase disruptor: 2x2 armor equipment that locks wild, wandering energy streams onto enemies within 15 tiles, with a coiling blue filament, impact sparks, a glowing tip and sound.
    - Every module fires its own stream; streams from the same suit are in phase and safe to cross.
    - Crossing the streams: streams from two different emitters on the same target cause a massive explosion (map setting).
    - Single stream mode for big suits or busy multiplayer maps (map setting).

Date: 2026-11-12

Version: 0.1.9
Date: 2026-11-12
  Optimizations:
    - Streams redraw every other tick, staggered so half update on even ticks and half on odd.
    - Long streams cost the same as medium ones, and the filament uses far fewer points (WePlay: Beam Library 0.5.0).
    - Extra streams on the same target skip their own sparks, tip glow and light; the main stream still has them all.
  Changes:
    - Streams in progress when loading a save from an older version are cleared and come back on the next hit.

Version: 0.1.0
Date: 2026-11-12
  Features:
    - Phase disruptor: 2x2 armor equipment that automatically locks a wandering energy stream onto enemies within 15 tiles.
    - The stream meanders and its tip circles the target, with a blue filament coiling around it and sparks spraying where it hits.
    - Switching targets swings the stream over instead of popping; a burst of sparks when a target dies.
    - 12 electric damage every 10 ticks per stream; 60 kJ per 20-tick shot from your suit. Laser research bonuses apply.
    - Unlocked by its own technology after Personal laser defense.
